YOUR QUICK GUIDE TO Phishing · Fake offers · Account scams · Online safety

01 / LEARN THE WARNING SIGNS

Scams often look ordinary.
Know what to look for.

Fraudulent messages may imitate trusted people or services. Look for patterns, not just spelling mistakes.

↗

01 — LINKS

Unexpected links

Check the full website address before opening a link. A familiar logo does not prove a message is genuine.

Check the destination
!

02 — URGENCY

Pressure to act fast

Threats such as “your account closes today” are designed to make you act before thinking.

Pause and verify
◇

03 — SECRETS

Requests for sensitive data

Never share passwords, OTPs, or banking PINs in response to a message or unexpected call.

Keep credentials private
₹

04 — OFFERS

Deals that feel too good

Unexpected prizes, refunds, jobs, or investment returns may be used to lure people into scams.

Verify independently
